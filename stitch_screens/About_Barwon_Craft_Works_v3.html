A Fusion of Heritage & Innovation

Barwon Craft Works began in a small workshop in Geelong with a simple lathe and a passion for local timber. Today, it stands as a testament to the marriage of old-world patience and new-world technology.

We don't just make pens; we craft heirlooms. By combining the tactile warmth of Australian hardwoods with the infinite possibilities of 3D printing, we create bespoke items that are as functional as they are beautiful.

Precision Turning

Each pen is hand-turned on a lathe in our Geelong workshop. Whether wood or resin, the material is shaped with an obsessive eye for perfect balance, weight, and ergonomic comfort.
